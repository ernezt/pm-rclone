Privacy policy

pm-rclone is used only by its owner, for backing up the owner's own files.

  • It requests only the drive.file scope, so it can access only files it created itself, not the rest of the Google Drive.
  • Files are uploaded from the owner's home server to the owner's own Google Drive. No data is sent to, stored by, or shared with anyone else.
  • No analytics, tracking, or third-party services are involved.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
